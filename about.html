About Us

We are the Senior Class of 2026, a group of students united by friendship, dedication, and the dream of graduating together. Throughout our journey, we have shared unforgettable experiences, supported one another, and grown both academically and personally.


Our Mission

To inspire each other to achieve academic excellence, strengthen teamwork, and leave a positive impact on our school community while preparing for our future.


Our Vision

To graduate as responsible, creative, and confident young adults who are ready to face new challenges and contribute positively to society.

  • Responsibility
  • Leadership
  • Teamwork
  • Perseverance

Class Motto
